Security Awareness Training

This domain is used for authorized security-awareness training — short, controlled phishing-simulation exercises that help people learn to recognize deceptive sign-in pages. It is operated privately for educational use and does not collect, log, or transmit credentials.

Arrived here from a training exercise?

Enter the campaign ID from your exercise to continue. No ID yet? The tips below apply to everyone.

  • Check the address bar: the real domain is the part just before the first single “/”.
  • Be wary of urgency (“your account will be closed!”) — it’s a classic pressure tactic.
  • Never enter a password reached from an email link; navigate to the site yourself.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication so a stolen password isn’t enough.
